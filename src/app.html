Questions students ask us

What is a "total package"?

It's everything you pay to move in for the first year: rent plus agreement, caution and agency fees. We show it as "First year". After that, you only pay the yearly rent, shown as "Every year after".

Why do some rooms say "Ask us"?

Some landlords only share the rent at first. We'd rather tell you we're confirming the full figure than show you a number that changes later. Request the room and we'll get it for you.

Is every room on this list still available?

Not always. It's rush hour and rooms are taken daily. The newest posts are shown first and are the most likely to still be free. We confirm availability before any inspection or payment.

Do I pay before I see the room?

No. You inspect first. If you can't come to Ife yet, we can arrange a live video inspection, or a trusted friend can inspect for you.

Can I share a room with a friend?

Yes, where the landlord allows it. Choose "Sharing" in the finder and we'll show each person's price and only rooms that can be shared.

Can my parents speak to you?

Of course. They can message or call us on WhatsApp at +234 913 641 6448.
